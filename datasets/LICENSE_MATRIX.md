# License and asset rights decisions

**13 October Hala model-rights note:** Qwen2-VL-2B, Qwen3-VL-2B/4B, Qwen3.5-4B, UI-TARS1.5-7B and Holo4-35B-A3B show Apache-2.0 model metadata at inspected revisions; ShowUI-2B and Fara1.5-4B/9B show MIT. The exact `Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct@6628554` [LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct/raw/66285546d2b821cf421d4f5eb2576359d3770cd3/LICENSE) is the **Qwen Research License**, non-commercial only without a separate commercial grant, despite an untagged model card. It is excluded from the commercial Hala shortlist. Check all component/model notices again before redistribution or hosting. Model permission never clears screenshots or training trajectories.

Weight/code/dataset metadata/raw asset/annotation/teacher output rights are **separate layers**. [Registry](DATASET_REGISTRY.md) gives per dataset details. No blanket commercial clearance is implied by public download, Apache code or MIT model metadata.

| Class | Initial disposition | Necessary evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Owned data or independently documentedCC0/PD | Approved under conditions | Owner/creator authority, file hash, license version/source capture, privacy/likeness/music checks, jurisdiction forPD |
| CCBY footage / specified Blender film | Approved under conditions | Asset level creator+attribution+license text and all third party material exceptions. Do not automatically approve every Commons asset |
| FineVideo/OpenVid/MiraData/Real Estate/Open VE | Requires review | Source asset grants, access agreement, teacher/metadata obligations, removal and privacy work flow |
| AMASS/Ego 4D under research terms / Panda unclear ed footage | Research-only | Separate compatible commercial grant before commercial use; no agreement accepted now |
| Benchmark tasks/trajectories, perfrequestsforSFT | Excluded | Scientific evaluation integrity or lack of gold targets; a copyright license cannot override this gate |
| Talking faces without cleared rights / copied film clips | Excluded | Explicit contract/consent and asset level permission required before any reconsideration |

MiraData GPL metadata does not automatically prove weights must be GPL, nor that GPL metadata licenses a YouTube video. Both assertions are unsupported without legal analysis. CC-BY-SA derivative-model effects are unresolved for our policy; excludeS A from the first pool rather than invent a universal rule. H3FAQconflictswithLICENSE, LTX2.xrevenuethresholds/transfer clauses and Cog commercial registration are material license gates. Model Apache licenses remain separate from upstream training data provenance, which maybe undisclosed.

Rights decisions are made by an authorized human reviewer and stored with the exact asset and contract revision. This plan does not accept agreements, purchase licenses or certify legal compliance. Approval expiration, withdrawalsandtakeddowns change dataset versions; freeze access/rebuild affected caches and checkpoint lineage before future use.
