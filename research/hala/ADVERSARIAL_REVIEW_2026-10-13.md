# Adversarial review of the Hala and Plume recommendations

This is a documented challenge to the research route, not an independent model execution. It uses a separate evidence reading of pinned model cards, Aladdin source, dataset cards and the proposed experiment. Reviewer role: attempt to falsify each recommendation before spend. No external reviewer or GPU run is claimed.

| Leading idea | Strongest counterargument | Evidence that would reverse or narrow the choice |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen3.5-4B as general Flash pilot | Vendor-reported OSWorld-Verified 35.6 may depend on an undisclosed harness; its generic action prompting may underperform already post-trained ShowUI or Fara. Arabic *text* scores do not prove RTL GUI grounding. | Same-harness, same-budget sealed desktop/browser/Arabic tasks. Choose ShowUI for grounding or Fara for English browser if they dominate their eligible strata. |
| Holo4 as Pro | Holo's 35B total weights cost far more to keep resident. Existing publisher trajectory success is much weaker under binary recomputation than a headline partial-completion score. Qwen3.5-4B or Fara9B may win cost-adjusted completion. | Paired fresh tasks with all failures retained, exact adapter and postcondition checks. Replace Holo if competitor meets multi-interface and safety gates at lower cost per success. |
| Flash+Pro hybrid | Escalation can double model calls, add queue delay and lose action history; a Pro-only worker may be simpler and cheaper when warmed. | Compare three arms (Flash-only, Pro-only, hybrid), include idle/cold cost and p95/p99 complete-task latency. Kill routing if it fails noninferiority or cost thresholds. |
| Two LoRAs | Shared base limits, adapter switching and domain separation may reduce quality; multitask learning or unchanged released agent could be better. | Equal-budget single LoRA, two LoRA and no-training controls on unseen apps/languages. No second adapter without statistically useful gain. |
| 500 screenshots | Correlated layouts/augmentations can overfit coordinates. Short trajectories, negative examples and recovery may matter more. | Group-split learning curves at 100/200/500 *independent* labeled screens plus new tasks; stop when validation gain saturates. |
| Harness-first | Deterministic UIA/DOM cannot resolve ambiguous visual intent; model post-training could be necessary. | Freeze harness and isolate genuine model gain. If no harness gains on difficult tasks, invest in rights-cleared trajectory SFT. |
| Wan2.2 for Plume | A newer unrestricted model such as LongCat may win at less tuning; Wan lacks some native conditioning/audio capabilities and high+low expert weights complicate memory. | Equal-resolution/cost zero-training bake-off, plus conditioning audit; switch if a challenger has better blind quality and equal rights. |
| PB01 video LoRA | Source-level BF16 compatibility is not runtime proof. Video memory/throughput may exceed the budget, and synthetic camera labels may not transfer to real footage. | PB00 actual optimizer/save/load and remote GPU quote, then sealed real-domain paired evaluation. Stop before 1,000 steps if throughput/memory or real-domain signals fail. |
| Dataset rights | Wrapper licenses do not clear every embedded video, sound, logo, person or UI screenshot. Benchmark task traces contaminate evaluation. | Asset-level admission records and task/source denylist. Reject ambiguous assets; regenerate synthetic alternatives. |

The most damaging open uncertainty is **complete-task measurement**. Neither Hala nor Plume has an independently executed baseline. The plan therefore recommends experiment *order* and stop criteria, not quality leadership.
