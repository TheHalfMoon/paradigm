Fara's arithmetic lead does **not** qualify it for desktop tasks it does not document. Hard capability gates precede weights. For browser-only deployment, Fara 9B is the leading challenger; test 27B when quality dominates latency. For general desktop/MCP retain Holo 35 until a challenger passes that coverage gate. Scores can change with browser/desktop workload composition; publish both axes.

Route A(tune Holo directly) incurs 70GBbase plus optimizer/activation burden before harness correctness is known. RouteB(replace with Fara) is plausible for browser-only scope, unproven broad desktop substitute. RouteC has lower uncertainty and fast fresh-task feedback. RouteD uses a small grounding/browser specialist plus Holo orchestration, but routing must improve cost without introducing silent protocol errors; defer until monolithic baseline measured.

Switch when a challenger exceeds Holo on the same frozen fresh task suite with 95%paired task-cluster bootstrap lower bound on success difference>0, no safety or unseen-app regression, and at least 30%lower cost/success **or** ≥5 percentage-point success improvement. Require desktop/MCP coverage for a full switch; permit separate browser deployment if a general switch is unjustified.
