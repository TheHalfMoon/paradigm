# Hala recommended research route

**Decision:** Plume executes first. For Hala, test a general Flash candidate (`Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B@851bf6e`) and a Pro control (`Hcompany/Holo4-35B-A3B@5084587`) in an Aladdin-compatible, policy-bound harness. Test `microsoft/Fara1.5-4B@776a33a` in the browser lane and `showlab/ShowUI-2B@cabec4f` as a small grounding control. Qwen2-VL-2B remains the proposal baseline. These are exact, publicly available checkpoints with inspectable license metadata; their performance in Aladdin is **unmeasured**. [Architecture and candidate matrix](HALA_FLASH_VS_HOLO4_ARCHITECTURE.md).

The highest-leverage first action is a frozen-model, fresh-task comparison that separates the benefit of deterministic UIA/DOM-first execution, state verification and recovery from a weight change. The provisional Holo Pro base has the broadest inspected desktop/browser/MCP training intent, but its 35B total weights impose a roughly 70 GB BF16 floor and publisher benchmark metrics are not independent task replications. Qwen3.5-4B has vendor visual-agent and multilingual evidence, not verified Arabic GUI or our own success. Fara1.5-4B has strong vendor browser completion evidence but explicitly English-only training and a browser scope. Released ShowUI already supplies a 2B GUI action model, so a custom Qwen2-VL-2B LoRA must beat that baseline to justify data and GPU work.

Use the [120-task Flash first experiment](../../plans/HALA_FLASH_FIRST_EXPERIMENT.md) and [evaluation protocol](../../benchmarks/EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md). Keep official OSWorld 2.1, BrowserGym/WebArena/VisualWebArena and Hcompany traces as versioned external evaluation/analysis lanes, not training material. Build licensed synthetic tasks with success, failure, negative/no-action and recovery states. Preserve rights/provenance and app/template-disjoint splits. [Dataset forensics](DATASET_FORENSICS.md) and [2B feasibility](SMALL_MODEL_FEASIBILITY.md) detail why 10,000 OSWorld traces and 10,500 resized variants cannot be counted as independent safe training examples.

The intended integration is `intent → founder-hosted Aladdin planner/router → Hala proposal → local qdral policy/approval/execution → fresh observation and verifier`. This follows the public [Aladdin source-grounded proposal](HALA_FOR_ALADDIN.md), not an invented Desktop Commander API. Direct typed tools take precedence when reliable; Flash handles eligible low-risk visual proposals; Pro handles ambiguity/complexity/recovery only if matched trials justify escalation. Local/offline GGUF is a research branch that requires an explicit change to Aladdin's adopted hosted-AI direction.

Training is deferred until a diagnosed residual error remains. Compare unchanged model, harness-only, one multitask LoRA and two specialized LoRAs under equal data/steps/parameters. No claimed six-hour Kaggle result, 0.2-second click, 1.2 GB full deployment, $0.00002 action or $5 subscription is treated as measured. [Unit economics](../../docs/COST_AND_COMPUTE.md) exposes idle and support sensitivity. [Adversarial review](ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW_2026-10-13.md) identifies decisive ways this route could fail.

**Switch/stop rules:** no unauthorized effects; no cross-benchmark contamination. Route Flash only for task strata whose paired success is noninferior to Pro within the preregistered margin and whose measured cost per successful task is materially lower. Replace Holo Pro only when a challenger matches desktop/browser/MCP scope and wins fresh task success/safety/cost at the same execution budget. Stop training after one bounded pilot if held-out unseen-app/Arabic gains vanish or hardware qualification fails. The current result is an implementation-ready *experiment plan*, not a demonstrated Hala model.
