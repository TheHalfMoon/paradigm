# Executive answers: Paradigm research, 13 October 2026

These are evidence-backed *research decisions*, not measured model quality. No Plume/Hala weights were loaded, no GPU training or official benchmark was run, and no subscription economics were observed. Pinned source identities and claim limits appear in the [source registry](../research/evidence/source-registry.json), [master plan](MASTER_PLAN.md) and [validation report](VALIDATION_REPORT.md).

1. **Hala architecture:** test a Flash/Pro route against both single-model controls. Deterministic tool/semantic selection and the Aladdin local authority layer remain mandatory. Do not deploy a hybrid until it improves verified success-adjusted cost without safety or p95 latency regression. [Architecture](../research/hala/HALA_FLASH_VS_HOLO4_ARCHITECTURE.md).
2. **Flash checkpoint:** `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B@851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a` is the provisional *general desktop* pilot, not a proven winner. Compare Qwen2-VL-2B, ShowUI-2B and browser-specialized Fara1.5-4B under the same harness. Arabic UI is a required held-out stratum.
3. **Pro checkpoint:** `Hcompany/Holo4-35B-A3B@508458727831368da85d53a8055c2b0f923ed4f3` remains the provisional broad desktop/browser/MCP control. Its roughly 70 GB BF16 weight floor, vendor-only performance and public-index metric ambiguity make Fara, UI-TARS and Qwen alternatives serious challengers. [Holo analysis](../research/hala/HOLO4_DEEP_DIVE.md).
4. **2B training feasibility:** a narrow GUI-grounding LoRA pilot is plausible after hardware qualification; a broadly capable Aladdin agent from 500 screenshots has no evidence. T4 time, vision/QLoRA compatibility, optimizer/save and quantized quality are untested. [Audit](../research/hala/SMALL_MODEL_FEASIBILITY.md).
5. **Proposal claims:** the Qwen2-VL-2B checkpoint and Apache license are verified; its BF16 files total 4.418 GB. The 0.2-second click, $0.00002/action, six-hour Kaggle training, ~1.2 GB working GGUF and $5 subscription are unverified. Augmentation cannot create 10,500 independent screens. Public OSWorld-Verified trajectories are benchmark evaluations, not 10,000 safe training samples. Two LoRAs must beat a single multitask adapter.
6. **Shortest useful Hala prototype:** version 120 synthetic browser/Windows tasks, adapt released frozen models to Aladdin's untrusted action schema, use typed semantic tools where qualified, and independently verify final state. No training is required to answer whether a useful baseline exists. [First experiment](../plans/HALA_FLASH_FIRST_EXPERIMENT.md).
7. **Shortest credible competitive Hala:** fix harness failures; collect licensed success/failure/recovery trajectories only for diagnosed deficits; run matched frozen-model and LoRA ablations on unseen apps, Arabic/RTL, official benchmarks and adversarial tasks. Require repeated sealed success, safety and cost evidence. World-class status is not established.
8. **Plume base:** `Wan2.2-T2V-A14B` is the best *first* commercially usable camera-control foundation with inspected Apache weight rights and a source-qualified expert LoRA route. Compare MIT LongCat-Video under equal outputs before claiming the best overall model. [Comparison](../research/plume/MODEL_COMPARISON.md).
9. **Plume first data:** proposed 1,200 procedural single-shot camera clips using owned/CC0 inputs, grouped 1,000 train / 100 dev / 100 sealed. FineVideo, Wikimedia, Blender and NASA are later candidates only after per-asset image/music/person rights and provenance review. No pilot assets have been ingested. [Dataset landscape](../research/plume/DATASET_LANDSCAPE.md).
10. **Musubi correction:** source at `f8a1b03794a49239a3539015075f5123d6c07d66` rejects FP16 DiT with BF16 mixed precision. PB01 points to original BF16 Wan shard 1 of 6 through the inspected expansion loader and uses BF16. The old FP16-repack paragraph was removed. Source compatibility is confirmed; actual load, optimizer, save and video improvement are pending PB00. [Compatibility report](../research/plume/MUSUBI_COMPATIBILITY_REPORT.md) and [recipe](../experiments/plume/PB01/README.md).
11. **First execution experiment:** Plume **PB00** memory/load/cache/gradient/export qualification, then PB01 high-noise camera LoRA only if PB00 passes. Hala's first model experiment follows; Hala task specs may be prepared without GPU now.
12. **Budget:** zero external cash for research preparation; an illustrative first Plume cycle assumes 50–144 A100 80 GB GPU-hours, roughly $70–$200 at an inspected $1.39/hour snapshot, excluding human review, storage and other costs. Hala $5/$20 margins are sensitivity examples, not forecasts; warm GPU idle alone can make $5 unviable at low subscriber count. [Compute and economics](COST_AND_COMPUTE.md).
13. **Blocked:** actual asset rights and HF capacity, exact remote GPU quote/approval, model loading and measured throughput, benchmark agreement where required, Aladdin integration authorization, Arabic/desktop task results, and hosting/privacy/business validation. No paid GPU or cloud provisioning was used.
14. **Committed research:** source registry, Plume/Hala comparisons, dataset rights, REA investigation, validation tools, PB01 package, Hala small-model/architecture/Aladdin/economics/experiment reviews, roadmap and decision log. Exact commit list and validation state are in [current status](CURRENT_STATUS.md) and the PR history, not inferred from this prose.
15. **Canonical GitHub SHA:** record the verified `main` commit after the research PR merges. A branch or local SHA is not canonical main. [Current status](CURRENT_STATUS.md) gives the recovery checkpoint but does not predeclare merge success.
